95 //! * if \f$p < q + 1\f$: the series converges absolutely for \f$z\in \mathbb{C}\f$, but can be very hard to compute accurately

98 //! summation stops, an \f$a_i\f$ or \f$b_i\f$ being a non positive flint and the polynomial is so defined everywhere.
99 //! 2. With the regularized option the result is divided by the product of the \f$\Gamma(b_i)\f$ extended to the elements of the `b` tuple.
100 //!
101 //! Up to now the only implemented functions are for size of `a` and `b` tuples running from 0 to 2. And some can have flaws.
102 //!
103 //! @note hypergeometric functons are a kind of jungle. As **KYOSU** and **EVE** only use standard floating types as base of computations,
104 //! overflows or lack of precision along large computation or huge values can degrade the accuracy.
105 //! Moreover:
106 //! - These fonctions always return complex outputs even if entries are all real ones.
107 //! - The use of float-based inputs and computations are ok, but discouraged as overflows or lack of precision are much greater
108 //! than double-based ones.
109 //! - receiving a `fnan` ouput generally means than the computation did not end well.
110 //! - receiving a `cinf` ouput generally means than the function is not defined here and that point is a singularity.
111 //!
